## ISO/IEC 23894 — AI Risk Management Standard

**ISO/IEC 23894** is an international standard published by the **International Organization for Standardization (ISO)** and the **International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC)**. Here's a comprehensive overview:

---

### 📋 What Is It?

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Full Title** | ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — *Information Technology – Artificial Intelligence – Guidance on Risk Management* |
| **Published** | February 2023 |
| **Developed by** | ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 42 (Subcommittee on Artificial Intelligence) |
| **Type** | Guidance standard (not a certifiable standard like ISO 27001) |

---

### 🎯 Purpose & Scope

ISO/IEC 23894 provides **guidance on how organizations can manage risks specific to the development, deployment, and use of AI systems**. It is designed to be used in conjunction with an organization's broader risk management framework (e.g., ISO 31000).

It covers:
- Establishing AI-specific risk management processes
- Identifying, analyzing, evaluating, and treating risks associated with AI
- Integrating AI risk management into organizational governance
- Addressing harms to individuals, groups, and society arising from AI systems

---

### 🧩 Key Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **AI Risk** | The potential for harm or negative consequences from AI system failures, biases, misuse, or unintended behaviors |
| **Risk Treatment** | Identifying options to modify AI-related risks (mitigate, avoid, share, accept) |
| **Stakeholder Involvement** | Engaging affected parties in the AI risk process |
| **AI Lifecycle** | Risk management applied across design, development, testing, deployment, and decommissioning |
| **Transparency & Accountability** | Ensuring AI decisions can be explained and responsibility is assigned |

---

### 🔗 Relationship to Other Standards & Frameworks

ISO/IEC 23894 is part of a growing **AI standards ecosystem**:

| Related Standard | Role |
|-----------------|------|
| **ISO 31000:2018** | Parent risk management framework — 23894 is an AI-specific extension |
| **ISO/IEC 42001:2023** | AI Management System (AIMS) — the certifiable AI governance standard |
| **ISO/IEC 22989** | AI concepts and terminology |
| **ISO/IEC 24368** | AI ethics and societal concerns |
| **NIST AI RMF 1.0** | U.S. equivalent AI risk framework (parallel, not identical) |
| **EU AI Act** | EU regulation that references risk-based AI governance (aligned in spirit) |

---

### 🏢 Who Should Care About It?

- Organizations **developing or deploying AI/ML systems**
- Companies subject to the **EU AI Act** (ISO/IEC 23894 can support compliance)
- Any organization with **automated decision-making** systems
- Industries like **healthcare, financial services, critical infrastructure** using AI

---

### 📌 SCF Relevance

ISO/IEC 23894 is **not directly listed as a named framework mapping** in SCF 2026.2, but its requirements are addressed through several SCF domains and controls, particularly those mapped to the **EU AI Act** and AI-related guidance:

- **TDA (Trusted Design & Architecture)** — AI system design and data governance
- **RSK (Risk Management)** — AI-specific risk assessment *(RSK-06.1)*
- **GOV (Governance)** — AI governance and accountability
- **EMB (Embedded Technology)** — Safe and autonomous system operations *(EMB-19)*
- **PRI (Data Privacy)** — Privacy-by-design for AI *(PRI-04.3)*

---

### ✅ Bottom Line

ISO/IEC 23894 is essentially the **international playbook for AI risk management** — it tells organizations *how* to think about and manage AI-related risks systematically. While it's guidance (not certification), it's increasingly referenced in regulatory contexts, especially the **EU AI Act**.
